01 — THE QUESTION

What happens to a machine when its mission ends?

Not every space explorer comes home. Some machines remain on distant worlds after their missions are over.

From the dusty surface of the Moon to the rocky landscapes of Mars, these forgotten explorers remind us that every discovery has a story.

03 — THE RED PLANET

Far from home.
Never out of history.

MARS EXPLORATION
NASA / SOJOURNER
MARS PATHFINDER · 1997

Sojourner

Small but brave! Sojourner was the first successful Mars rover. It was planned to work for only seven days, but it explored Mars for 83 days.

MISSION Mars Pathfinder
STATUS Silent

NASA / SPIRIT
MARS EXPLORATION · 2004

Spirit

Spirit was designed to explore Mars for 90 days. Instead, it continued working for years, discovering evidence of an ancient watery environment.

MISSION Mars Exploration

NASA / OPPORTUNITY
MARS EXPLORATION · 2004

Opportunity

Opportunity was expected to last 90 days. It kept exploring for almost 15 years and traveled more than 45 kilometers across the Martian surface.
